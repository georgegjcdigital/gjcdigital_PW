I am a technology and digital transformation professional with experience across enterprise technology, AI adoption, workflow automation, governance, and operational transformation.

With experience contributing to digital transformation initiatives across multiple industries, I bring a practical understanding of enterprise technology, operational governance, service delivery, and AI-driven transformation.

I help organisations

  • Modernise operations and automate workflows

Most digital transformation efforts fall short not because of the technology — but because organisations rush to adopt AI without the governance, the people strategy, or the operational foundations to make it stick. I believe responsible AI is not a constraint on progress — it is a competitive advantage. And real transformation only happens when it is practical, adopted, and built around the people it is meant to serve.
